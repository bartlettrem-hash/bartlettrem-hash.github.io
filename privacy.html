Privacy Policy

The Spellbook VR uploader is used only by the game's developer, on their own computer, with their own TikTok account.

It asks TikTok for permission to upload videos (video.upload). It uses that permission only to send gameplay videos to the developer's own TikTok inbox as drafts.

It does not collect, sell or share personal data. The login token TikTok returns is stored only on the developer's own computer and can be removed at any time by revoking access in TikTok's settings.
